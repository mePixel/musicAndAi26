Huge sculptural luminous pink curved stage architecture, dramatic diagonal white spotlights and a punchy lemon-yellow platform, joyful rather than dark. UI layout: tiny wordmark top left, massive condensed title "OWN THE FLOOR" across the upper backdrop, dancer centered in a generous theatrical space, two wide compact track tickets across bottom with selected ticket popping forward, and oversized hot pink call-to-action right of tickets. Secondary controls quiet along bottom edge. Graphic flat UI over premium dimensional stage, clear and intentional. This should look like opening a dance party game, not reading a website.
