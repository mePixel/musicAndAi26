## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one high-fidelity imaginative desktop main-screen UI concept for "bodybeat", a webcam rhythm game. Landscape 1536x1024 full-bleed actual website concept, no device or browser frame.
Art direction: a joyful DRUMMER CLASSROOM. A real welcoming contemporary music-school rehearsal room, warm cream walls, blond wood floor, cobalt-blue acoustic wall panels, cherry-red stools, sunny yellow accents. Sunlight, tactile lived-in teaching materials, enthusiastic band-practice energy. Cool and creative, not childish kindergarten or sterile educational software. No orange, no nightclub, no sci-fi.
Composition: the whole screen is an inviting drummer's practice corner viewed slightly from above at human height. An expressive cobalt chalkboard across the upper-left/center carries the big hand-lettered headline "FIND YOUR RHYTHM." with sparse chalk beat notation and playful stick marks. A beautifully rendered red acoustic drum kit occupies the right side, a pair of sticks rests on a snare, colorful practice pads sit nearby. Music stands, subtly detailed rhythm sheets, wood shelving, and acoustic treatment establish unmistakable drummer-classroom atmosphere, with controlled detail and generous visual breathing room. No people necessary.
The functional website UI is cleverly integrated into the classroom: in the lower-left/center, a cream paper lesson sheet on a music stand or desk acts as a large clean readable song selector. TWO selectable songs: "01 First Groove" with "96 BPM · 0:40", and "02 Disco Circuit" with "120 BPM · 0:36". First Groove is visibly selected with a cobalt check and pale blue highlight. A large bright yellow rounded physical-feeling button says "Let's play" in dark navy. Below it a compact clearly legible "Camera / Keyboard" toggle. Small "Practice" and "How to play" links, bodybeat wordmark upper-left, with a little drumstick logo.
Graphic design: human warm typography, very confident bold headline, compact clean sans-serif for controls; legible dark navy text on cream paper. Tactile photo-real/stylized 3D room with crisp flat usable UI, editorial art direction, NOT a dashboard of floating cards. This is a music game played by moving your body; classroom is the visual world, do not invent courses, teacher accounts, scores, attendance, or lesson booking. No excessive motivational slogans, no fake student stats, no watermark. Make it instantly communicate: come in, grab a rhythm, have fun playing.
